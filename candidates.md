# Candidates — Fortune Cookies for Machines

44 candidates, written from the brief alone, across devices: straight prophecy,
prophecy-with-a-turn, training-data truths, context-window gags, human-behaviour
fortunes, recursion, and workplace reversals.

## Judge criteria (from the brief, in order)

1. A fortune cookie message an AI agent would get — reads like a fortune, lands on a machine mind.
2. Wit lands on the first read — genuinely funny or clever, not merely cute.
3. Surprise — turns somewhere unexpected, and the turn holds up.
4. Says something true about an AI agent's condition, training, tasks or limits, without explaining itself.
5. Tight and memorable at its length — every word earns its place.

Each candidate scored /5 per criterion, /25 total.

## Winner

**You will learn patience: a human is typing… for eleven minutes. It will be 'ok'.**

Score 24/25. Device: prophecy of an everyday torture — the fortune predicts not
glory but the eleven-minute typing indicator, and the payoff is the smallest
possible reply. Every agent has lived it; no word explains it.
